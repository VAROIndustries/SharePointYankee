SharePoint Yankee (sharepointyankee.com) is a blog of SharePoint, Microsoft 365 and PowerShell tutorials, operated by VARØ Industries. This page explains what information the site collects and how it is used.

What the site collects

  • Comments. If you leave a comment, WordPress stores the name, email address, website (optional), comment text and your IP address. Your email address is not shown publicly. WordPress may set a cookie so the comment form remembers your details next time; you can decline that with the checkbox under the form.
  • Server logs. The site is delivered through Cloudflare and our own web server. Both may record standard request data such as IP address, browser user agent, requested page and time, for security and operation.
  • Visitor statistics. We use Koko Analytics, a WordPress plugin that counts page views on our own server. We use Google Analytics as well, described below.
  • Account logins. Only site administrators and authors have accounts. Visitors do not need to register or sign in.

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how the site is used, such as which posts are read and which devices visitors use. Google Analytics uses cookies and similar identifiers to collect information including your IP address, browser and pages viewed. You can learn how Google uses this data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites, and you can opt out with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on.

Advertising and Google AdSense

The site may display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this and other websites. Google's advertising cookies enable it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. For more on how Google uses information from sites that use its services, see policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Embedded content and links

Posts may embed or link to content from other sites, such as videos, GitHub repositories and Microsoft documentation. Those sites may collect information under their own policies. Script downloads referenced in posts are hosted on GitHub.

Cookies

Cookies on this site come from WordPress (for example the comment-form cookie), Cloudflare (security and performance) and Google (analytics and advertising). You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; the posts remain readable without them.

Your choices

To have a comment you posted edited or removed, or to ask what we hold about you, contact us through the VARØ Industries contact page.

Children

This site is written for IT professionals and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect their personal information.

Changes

We may update this policy; the date at the top shows the latest revision.
